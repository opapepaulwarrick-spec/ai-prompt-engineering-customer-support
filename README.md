1-"My order was supposed to arrive Tuesday. It's Friday. Where is it?? This is unacceptable."

--The customer placed an order scheduled for delivery on Tuesday but is experiencing a delay of more than two days. He adds that this situation is unacceptable to him.

Response from prompt: Claude AI


Hello,

I completely understand your frustration: a delivery scheduled for Tuesday that still hasn't arrived by Friday is a significant delay, and I sincerely apologize for this.

To find out exactly where your package is, I need a few details:

Your order number (or the email address used for the purchase)
The delivery address listed on the order, so I can verify it is correct
Once I have this information, I will check the shipment status with the carrier and let you know exactly where your package is and when it will arrive.

Depending on what I find, here is what I can do:

Package delayed in transit: I will follow up with the carrier and provide you with a reliable new delivery date.
Package stuck or lost: I will immediately launch an investigation and, depending on your preference, either send a replacement order via priority shipping or issue a full refund.
Address issue: I will correct the information and arrange a new delivery attempt.
In any case, I will ensure the issue is resolved. Could you please send me your order number so I can look into this right away?

Best regards,
Your Customer Support Agent

Hello,
Yes, most likely. Your order was placed just 10 minutes ago, so it almost certainly hasn't been prepared or handed over to the carrier yet. This is the best time to change the address, as the process becomes much more complicated once the package has shipped.
To proceed right away, please send me:
Your order number (or the email address used for the purchase)
The full new address, including the neighborhood or district, a landmark if necessary, and a phone number where the delivery driver can reach you
Once I receive this, I will check the order status:
If it is not yet being prepared: I will update the address immediately and send you written confirmation with the new address.
If it is already being prepared: I will contact the warehouse to try to halt the process and correct the address before shipment.
If it has already shipped: I will ask the carrier to redirect the package. This depends on the carrier and may affect the delivery time, but I will keep you updated.
If the address change affects shipping costs or delivery times (for example, if it involves a different city), I will let you know before finalizing anything.
I am ready to assist as soon as you send me this information.

Your Customer Support Agent
